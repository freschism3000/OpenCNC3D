Review notes

The frame, rails, corner plates, dark neutral field, font and button ramps come from the approved RA3D menu kit. Cameos, radar emblems, costs and prerequisites come from original Red Alert assets and rules. Five production categories include Ships. The left glass power meter reuses the original POWERBAR and POWER sprites; the right edge stays a plain rail.

Click to order; right-click removes a queued unit, then holds or cancels. Scroll the cameo list. Completed buildings need a click to enter placement and a click on the field. The tool wing has equal inner clearances and sits under the minimap. Controls also accept keyboard focus, Enter and arrow-key tab navigation.

Browser design review. The saved RA battlefield, radar, production timing, building-power switch and selection are fixtures; this screen does not run the game engine. Runtime integration follows review of this version.
