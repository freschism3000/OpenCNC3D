C&C 3D: THE MAIN MENU
=====================

Double-click PLAY-MENU.command.

The Westwood logo animation plays first and ends on the title screen, then the menu
fades up out of black and the music starts. Any key or mouse button skips a movie.

  Arrow keys        move the highlight
  Return, or click  activate
  Intro & Sneak Peek plays the full C&C intro (about two minutes, skippable)
  Exit Game, or ESC  quit

WHAT IS AND IS NOT WIRED UP
---------------------------
Start New Game, Load Mission and Multiplayer Game print their name and do nothing yet.
There is no faction select and no mission list behind them. Exit works.

To play an actual mission, use PLAY-GDI.command or PLAY-NOD.command as before: the
menu is not yet the way into the game.

WHERE IT ALL COMES FROM
-----------------------
This screen is pure MS-DOS, no N64 content, except the title plate, which is the
CNC3D artwork. Layout, colours and draw order are taken from the GPL Tiberian Dawn
sources line by line, and the art, fonts and button labels are decoded out of the
1995 discs. The movies are the 1995 CD's own VQA files (dosdata/movies/), decoded by
our own player; the music is the original .AUD score. Nothing on this screen, movies
and audio included, comes from the N64 cartridge.

FILES THIS ADDS
---------------
  cnc_menu            the menu (SDL2 + OpenGL 1.1, same as cnc_eyes)
  dosmenu.pack        title plate, fonts, filigree, mouse pointer
  movies/LOGO.VQA     the logo animation that plays before the menu
  movies/INTRO2.VQA   the intro film
  music/*.AUD         the score. MAP1 is the menu theme the engine asks for

Nothing else in the folder is touched. Everything is path relative: keep the folder
together and it runs from anywhere.
