# THE REFINERY'S UNLOAD RIG MUST NOT PLAY WHILE THE REFINERY IS BEING BUILT.
#
# Reported: placing a Refinery immediately played the Harvester unload animation,
# which it should not.
#
# The refinery is the one type in the cartridge's table whose arm emits TWO model
# commands: the building, and slot 20 -- a six-node rig with a one-shot clip -- for
# stages 12..28 of the engine's refinery animation. Nothing in the arm asked whether the
# building EXISTS yet. A building under construction is BState 0 (BSTATE_CONSTRUCTION),
# its stage counter is already running, and so the rig played on top of the buildup.
#
# WHY G31d COULD NOT SEE THIS. G31d runs SCG90EA, where the refinery is already standing:
# 39 samples, BStates 1, 3 and 4 only, not one sample in BState 0 -- and its awk never
# reads the bstate field at all. A gate can only fail on a state its scenario reaches.
# This script exists to reach that state: --build 6 gives the credits, and the refinery is
# built, placed, and then watched tick by tick THROUGH its construction.
#
# THE WINDOW IS THE ANTI-VACUITY LEG. gates.sh counts the samples with bstate==0 AND
# stage>=12 -- the samples where the arm's formula WOULD draw the rig -- and requires at
# least one. Without that floor, a scenario, a build level or a credit change that stops
# producing a placed refinery would leave zero samples, zero of them bad, and a green
# gate measuring nothing. Measured 21 samples in the window, 21 of them
# drawing the rig before the fix and 0 after it.
#
# `look` then `clickobj` pins the camera on the placed refinery and reads its silhouette
# out of THIS run, which is the crop the pixel leg differences inside. Nothing moves the
# camera after that: the pixel leg's whole claim is that those frames are IDENTICAL.
