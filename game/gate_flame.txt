# THE FLAMETHROWER'S TONGUE. Reported against v0.5.7, with G50 green: the Flame Trooper
# still showed no real fire when shooting -- a small orange particle was visible, but not
# the fire effect the N64 version draws.
#
# He was seeing exactly one particle per shot. The cartridge's FLAME-* animations have no
# SHP art, so the engine drops them after a single tick and the renderer's emitter, which
# is clocked off the anim's stage, fired once and stopped. The fix gives an art-less anim
# a clock of its own out of the brain's `biggest` field: ten emissions per shot, stages
# 0..9, all in ENGINE ticks so a replay stays byte-reproducible.
#
# WHY G50 COULD NOT SEE IT. It asserted `FLTHROW live >= 1` and `drawn >= live`. One
# particle satisfies both. A count that is ten times low is invisible to a floor of one,
# and it stayed green through the whole of this bug.
#
# The two clickobj lines are the pixel leg's anchors: the two troopers who fire in this
# window, read out of THIS run so the box follows them. clickobj also proves they are on
# the glass at all, which the particle counts alone would not.
cam 11 55
tick 449
shot shots/flame.png
clickobj E4 0
clickobj E4 1
efxdump
