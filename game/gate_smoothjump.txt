# G81 arm A: THE INTERPOLATOR'S DISCONTINUITY RULE.
#
# SCG01EA's GDIR1 wave is three riflemen aboard an LST. A passenger is LIMBOED, which
# takes it off the map but not out of the engine's dump, and a reinforcement passenger is
# never Unlimbo'd on the way in, so every tick of the voyage it reports the object
# constructor's sentinel coordinate: cell 255.996 on both axes. Frame 242 is the last tick
# with three men aboard and 243 the first with none.
#
# On 243 the drawn position must BE the engine's position. It used to be a filtered slide
# that started 254 cells away and took the length of the filter to arrive, which is a man
# flying in from off the map instead of stepping onto the beach.
#
# drawpos picks a unit by its POSITION among the riflemen, counting from 0, and a position
# is not an id. The riders are positions 15, 16 and 17 today; position 14 is a man who was
# never aboard. So the gate does not trust these numbers: it requires the three lines on
# 243 to name exactly the ids the 242 dump lists in limbo, and every trail line to belong
# to one of them, four lines each.
smoothmove 1
tickalpha 0.5
tick 242
cargodump
tick 1
cargodump
drawpos E1 15
drawpos E1 16
drawpos E1 17
tick 1
drawpos E1 15
drawpos E1 16
drawpos E1 17
tick 1
drawpos E1 15
drawpos E1 16
drawpos E1 17
tick 1
drawpos E1 15
drawpos E1 16
drawpos E1 17
tick 1
drawpos E1 15
drawpos E1 16
drawpos E1 17
quit
