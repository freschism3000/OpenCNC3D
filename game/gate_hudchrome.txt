# THE THREE WINDOW-PINNED PLATES. Three symptoms at once:
#   the OPTIONS button cannot be clicked, and the mouse cursor goes behind it;
#   the SIDEBAR text is baked into the texture instead of being real text;
#   the SIDEBAR does not act as a button that slides the whole panel to the right.
# All three failed for one reason: sb_over_panel defined the panel as the right-hand
# column, and these plates are pinned to the WINDOW. The click fell through to the map and
# the 1995 pointer was suppressed, so the cartridge's 3-D cursor drew in the world -- under
# the sidebar overlay, which is literally "the cursor goes behind it".
# THE UI SCALE IS PINNED AT 1x. Opening the dialog applies the dials, and the
# shipped default is -2x, which halves this bar at 960 rows and moves every plate this
# script probes by absolute pixel. The pixel-gate rule: pin every dial between the
# geometry and the glass, or the gate measures the art direction.
gfx ui_scale 0
tick 20
sbhit 160 17
sbhit 800 17
sbhit 1120 17
panelprobe 160 17
sbclick 160 17
shot shots/hud_open.png
sbslide hide
sbslide settle
shot shots/hud_hidden.png
sbhit 1120 17
sbslide show
sbslide settle
shot shots/hud_back.png
quit
