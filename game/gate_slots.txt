# each through the 1995 LoadOptionsClass box (loaddlg.cpp) as dosopt.c draws it, driven by

# `optrow N` presses the Nth visible row of the list. The SLOTS|open readouts are what the
# gate reads: the rows, the field, and whether the action button is live.
