# G109. THE SAM SITE MAKES A NOISE WHEN IT FIRES.
#
# This was asked for and turned out to already work; the gate exists so it keeps
# working, and so the next person to doubt it has a script that answers in 30 seconds.
#
# THREE THINGS ABOUT THIS SCRIPT ARE LOAD-BEARING. Each one, on its own, makes the run
# report `sfx=0/0` and look exactly like a dead sound:
#
#  1. THE LENGTH. A SAM only fires at AIRCRAFT, and the A-10s take roughly 250 engine
#     ticks from the strike request to reach the SAMs. The first version of this stopped
#     160 ticks after the click and saw nothing at all. Do not trim the ticks.
#  2. THE AIRCRAFT. SCG13EA carries four SAM sites and ZERO aircraft, so ticking it
#     forever proves nothing. The A-10s have to be summoned, which is what the super
#     weapon cheat and the air strike below are for. They are not decoration.
#  3. THE CLICK POINT. 738,437 is the SAM's own silhouette with the camera at 38,33.
#     A loose click puts the strike somewhere else and the SAMs never engage.
#
# The gate itself also passes --audiowav, for a fourth reason spelled out in gates.sh.
tick 60
cheatset super 1
tick 10
superrecharge
tick 10
cam 38 33
tick 5
sbclickitem SW_AirStrike
actclick 738 437
shot shots/g109_0.png
tick 60
shot shots/g109_1.png
tick 60
shot shots/g109_2.png
tick 60
shot shots/g109_3.png
tick 60
shot shots/g109_4.png
tick 60
shot shots/g109_5.png
tick 60
shot shots/g109_6.png
quit
