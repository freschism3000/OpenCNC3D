# SCB07EC pre-places a GDI base on three levels of ground: a Barracks at (45,52) on
# dead-flat 64, a Power Plant beside it at (47,51) whose ground reaches 87, a
# Construction Yard at (43,49) whose apron's second row lies on the Barracks' north
# edge, and a Refinery at (41,52) on 101. Under the old shared-corner rule the taller
# pad won every corner two buildings touched, so the Barracks was drawn at its own 64
# while its north edge read 76 and its east column 87: both quonset huts cut short
# and the plate sliced. padcheck turns that into numbers: every standing building's
# highest padded footprint corner against the ground its model is drawn on.
#
# The corner reads below are the Barracks' own south corners, which no neighbour
# touches, and its east column, which the plant shares. They must agree, because the
# building is one flat model and can only stand at one height.
